These rules match the app: the classes, powers, item sets and foe abilities below are drawn straight from the game itself.

The round

  1. Heroes act, in any order. Each hero gets a move and one action, in any order, and the move can be split around the action: step in, strike, step back out.
  2. The app keeps count: once you've attacked or used a power, your attack button and powers grey out until the GM ends the round. Wares are free and don't count. If the table agrees you should act again (a misclick, a ruling), the GM can give you another action from Headquarters.
  3. Enemy phase. The GM moves the foes, then taps Enemy phase. Every hero with foes on them rolls defense on their phone.
  4. End round. Everyone gains energy, Pins wear off, and foes with round abilities (Summoner, Regenerate) act.

Actions: Attack, use a Power, or Sprint (move again instead of acting).

Attack powers: a power whose text starts "Make an attack with…" (Thousand Cuts, Lantern Volley, Emberburst) is part of your attack: unleash it, then roll, all as your one action. Every other power is your whole action; any "+dice on your next attack" it gives carries to a later turn.

Moving

  • The board is a 1″ grid. Each square is 1″. Diagonal steps count as 1.
  • Your Move is shown on your sheet (Ironclad 5, Runepriest 5, Arcanist 5, Ranger 6, Shadowblade 7).
  • You can't move through foes. You can move through allies but can't end on them.
  • Difficult terrain (brambles, rubble, deep snow, anything the GM calls rough): each square costs 2 squares of movement. Foes are slowed the same way. Leaps, teleports and swaps ignore it.
  • Reach: melee callings attack adjacent foes only (including diagonals, and within 1″ of height). Ranged callings attack anything within their range that they can see.
  • If you are next to a foe and want to walk away, see Breaking away.

Height

  • Stairs and ramps cost normal movement.
  • Climbing a ledge or ladder costs 2 squares per 1″ of height.
  • Dropping down up to 2″ is free. Each inch beyond that deals 100 damage (the GM applies it).
  • High ground: attacking a foe that stands lower than you gives +1 die.
  • A foe striking you from higher ground adds +1 die against you when you defend.
  • Ledges are weapons. A foe pushed off a ledge or into a wall counts as slammed (see below).

Attacking

  • Tap Attack, roll your dice pool, and count the dice showing 4, 5 or 6. That's your pool of hits. Then say how many are 6s: those are crits.
  • Before rolling, tick what you've earned: High ground (+1 die) and Flanking (+1 die: an ally stands on the far side of your target).
  • Attacking from hiding adds +3 dice automatically, and ends your Hidden state.
  • Spend hits by tapping foes: a minion dies to one hit. An elite or boss takes your damage per hit, minus its armor. Crits hit big foes harder and are spent on them first.
  • No foes tracked in the app? Use Slay one on the table for each minion you cut down.
  • Cleave (from gear and synergies): each 6 you roll also gives you that many extra hits that can only be spent on minions. With +1 cleave, a roll with two 6s banks 2 bonus minion-only hits. They show up in your hit pool automatically; your normal hits are still spent on elites and bosses.
  • Unspent hits vanish at End round.

Powers & position

  • Powers cost energy. Many of them move you (leap, teleport, grapple, vanish) or move foes (push, pull, taunt).
  • Movement powers ignore free blows. That's how you get out of a crowd.
  • Do it on the table, then tap it in the app: move the minis first, then tap every foe the power caught. Tap a foe twice to mark it slammed.
  • Slammed: a foe pushed or pulled into a wall, another foe, or off a ledge takes +1 hit. Minions die.
  • Pushes go straight away from you; a foe stops if it hits something. Pulls drag foes next to the point.
  • Pinned foes can't move until End round. Taunted foes now fight the hero who taunted them.
  • Hidden heroes are ignored in the enemy phase. Moving and breaking away don't reveal you, but attacking does.

Breaking away

  • Walking away from foes that are next to you gives each of them a free blow (1 defense die each).
  • On your phone, tap Break away, choose the foes you're leaving, and roll the free blows. Those foes stop fighting you.
  • Teleports, leaps, grapples, swaps and being Hidden avoid free blows entirely.

Defending

  • In the enemy phase, roll one die per enemy attack. Each die showing 1, 2 or 3 is a blow that lands. With 30%+ armor, only 1 or 2 land.
  • Surrounded: with 3 or more foes on you, you roll +1 die.
  • A foe on higher ground: tick it on the defense prompt for +1 die.
  • Blows add up; your armor % is subtracted, then any ward soaks the rest.

Slams, falls & climbing out

  • Slam: if any blow lands from a foe with Slam, you're hurled 3″ straight away from it. Move your mini, then tell your phone where you landed. Open ground is free. Hitting a wall, a foe or an ally costs one more blow of damage, reduced by armour.
  • Falling in: going over an edge into a chasm or pit costs 10% of your max health. Armour doesn't help, but ward still absorbs it. Every foe on you loses its grip.
  • In the chasm: foes can't reach you, so the enemy phase skips you. You can't attack or use powers. Wares still work.
  • Climbing out is your action. Roll one die: 4+ gets you out. Each failed try adds +1 to the next, and an ally standing at the edge adds +1.

How foes move (GM)

  • Minions move 5″ toward the nearest hero they can reach and engage them.
  • Elites move 6″ toward whoever hurt them last.
  • Bosses move 6″ toward the hero with the most kills.
  • Pinned foes don't move. Foes don't chase heroes they can't see (Hidden).
  • New minions arrive at spawn points: doors, stairs, board edges.
  • In the app, a foe engaged with a hero means it's adjacent to them. Use the ⇄ and dropdown controls to update this as foes move.

Adventures: acts, scenes & pressure

  • The GM can run an adventure as acts made of scenes. Headquarters steps through them: Begin scene summons its foes, already sized, and later waves arrive on their own at End round.
  • Each scene has a pressure, where 1.0 is a fair fight. Pressure climbs through each act to its boss, and the next act starts easier again.
  • Foes are sized to the party as it was when the act began. Gear you find during an act makes the rest of that act easier. The boss's hoard is the big jump, and the next act measures you again.
  • Difficulty moves every scene's pressure: Casual −0.15, Brutal +0.2. Brutal also makes planned loot rarer and stronger.

Board snapshots (GM)

  • Print the markers from Board → Print markers. Put the four corner cards flush into the board's corners.
  • Every hero and every elite or boss has its own marker number (◰ on their card). Minions share generic markers #30–89.
  • Stick markers where the camera can see them from above: on the base beside the figure, or on a small flag above it. 0.75″ markers work best.
  • On Board, tap 📷 Photo (or use the live camera) with all four corners in view. The app finds every marker, places it on the grid, and updates the map on every phone.
  • Foes whose bases touch a hero (centres within 1.5″, 2″ for bosses) become engaged with that hero. Minions on the board replace the app's minion list.
  • Pieces that aren't seen keep their last state. Height isn't read from photos: the GM still judges high ground.

Energy

  • Gained for every kill (more for elites and bosses) and at every End round.
  • Spent on powers. A power glows on your phone when you can afford it.

Loot & item sets

  • There's no XP and no levels. You grow through loot. Scan treasure cards in chests and under elite and boss minis; one card can hold several items.
  • Compare and trade: tap any item to give it to an ally.
  • Set pieces (coloured): one equipped piece unlocks the set's power, and every extra piece strengthens it and may add bonuses.
  • Hidden synergies: wearing pieces from two different sets can unlock secret combinations. Your phone will tell you when you discover one.

The Rakshasa: merchant, boons & cards

  • A tiger-headed merchant turns up at rest points (the GM decides when). While his stall is open, a Visit the stall button appears on every sheet.
  • Selling: he buys items from your satchel (not what you're wearing) and pays in Favour ⟡. Sales are final. Favour you don't spend carries over to his next visit.
  • Boons are permanent stat bonuses, like gear that never takes a slot. Each one costs ⟡1 more every time you buy it again.
  • Wares are single-use consumables. They sit in the Wares row of your sheet; using one is free (not your action), one per turn. The Rakshasa also hands them out as rewards, so it pays to amuse him.

Three-Card Crown

  • The Rakshasa deals every challenged hero 3 cards and himself 3 face down. Suits: Swords, Flames, Crowns, Skulls, 1–9, plus two Rakshasa cards that count as any card.
  • On your phone, pick your wager (0–3 Favour) and tap up to 2 cards to swap, once. Then everyone reveals together, and he redraws his worst cards.
  • Hands, best first: Three of a kind › Run (three in a row, like 4-5-6) › Flush (one suit) › Pair › High card. Beat his hand to win your wager; lose it otherwise. Ties go to the house.
  • Sometimes he plays for a prize instead of Favour: beat him and the ware is yours.
