Adventure: acts & scenes

Lay out the adventure as acts of scenes. Each scene's foes are sized from a pressure curve: scenes climb from easy to hard through each act, the act's boss hoard lifts the party, and the next act starts easier again against the stronger party. Headquarters steps through the scenes and summons each one, wave by wave.

Elites and bosses: one per line, as Name x2 @0.5: enrage, regen | what it does. x2 = how many; @0.5 = size (health and damage). Abilities: . Pressure left blank follows the curve.
