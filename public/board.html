How it works

  • Print the markers. Put the four corner cards flush into the board's corners.
  • Each hero and each elite or boss has its own marker number (shown here and in Headquarters). Minions share generic markers 30–89.
  • Markers must face up and be visible from above: on the mini's base beside the figure, or on a small flag above it.
  • Take the photo from roughly above, with all four corner cards in view. Angles up to about 55° work.
  • Pieces that aren't seen keep their last state. Undo in Headquarters reverses a bad snap.
